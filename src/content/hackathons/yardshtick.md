## The hook

We gave a camera a sell button. It got us 3rd place.

## The weekend

**250+ participants** across **60 teams** joined **OpenAI Build Week Manila**, the first OpenAI hackathon in the Philippines.

We built **Yardshtick**: take one photo of a pile of stuff, and it detects every item, writes the listings, suggests prices, and turns the whole thing into a shareable garage sale.

## Under the hood

**GPT-5.6 Vision** to understand and identify the objects. **SAM 2** for pixel-level segmentation. AI-generated pricing and listing details to turn the whole scene into a storefront.

One photo. Multiple listings. Built in one day.
